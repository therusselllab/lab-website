Accessibility

Accessibility statement

This statement applies to the Russell Lab website (russelllab.org).

Our commitment

We are committed to making this website accessible to as many people as possible. We aim to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at Level AA.

What we do to make this site accessible

  • Use semantic HTML throughout, with appropriate heading structure
  • Provide text alternatives for non-text content
  • Ensure sufficient colour contrast ratios for all text
  • Design layouts that reflow correctly at 200% zoom
  • Support keyboard navigation throughout
  • Avoid content that flashes or moves without user control
  • Use descriptive link text throughout

Known limitations

We are not currently aware of any significant accessibility issues. If you find a problem, please let us know using the contact details below.

Feedback and contact

If you experience any difficulty accessing content on this site, or if you have suggestions for improvement, please contact us:

  • Email: andrew.russell@mrc-lms.ac.uk
  • Or use the contact form

We aim to respond to accessibility feedback within 5 working days.

Technical information

This website is built with plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. It does not use any frameworks that introduce known accessibility barriers. Third-party content (such as embedded maps) may not meet the same accessibility standards.

Enforcement procedure

The Equality and Human Rights Commission (EHRC) is responsible for enforcing the Public Sector Bodies (Websites and Mobile Applications) (No. 2) Accessibility Regulations 2018. If you are not happy with how we respond to your complaint, contact the Equality Advisory and Support Service (EASS).

He was then awarded an EMBO Postdoctoral Fellowship (2022) and the BroadIgnite award (2024) whilst in the laboratory of Dr. Fei Chen at the Broad Institute of MIT and Harvard, and the Harvard Department of Stem Cell & Regenerative Biology.

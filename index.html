Research

What we do

Our group combines genomics, single-cell approaches, and computational methods to understand how exposures — chemical, physical, and biological — leave marks on cells and tissues.

🧬

Technology Development

Building new experimental and computational tools to measure molecular phenotypes at scale across tissues and conditions.

Learn more →
🔬

Exposure Biology

Characterising how environmental, chemical, and lifestyle exposures alter gene expression, epigenetics, and cellular identity in specific tissues.

Learn more →
💻

Computational Analysis

Developing statistical frameworks and machine learning approaches to extract biological signal from high-dimensional molecular data.
